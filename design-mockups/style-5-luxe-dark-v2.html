About

Medical Informatics × AI
A Master's graduate from the AI track of Medical Informatics at Kaohsiung Medical University — I turn machine-learning ideas into deployed, real-world products.

During my internship I owned full-stack on a small product and led frontend on a larger one — wireframes, user flows, API integration, and shipping.

Backend: agentic workflows with LangGraph, JSON-prompt personas, and guardrails that keep malformed output from breaking the UI.

My proudest work: a complete AI system from scratch, applied to public issues like air pollution.
